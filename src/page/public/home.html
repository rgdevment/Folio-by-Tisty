Beta

Hand over what you know. Only people with the link can read it.

Your PO, the QA joining the project, another team: give them the document they need, not a summary pasted into a chat. Folio lets you share documents privately with anyone, using an encrypted link. You share from Tisty.

Get Tisty

Encrypted before it leaves, signed by you

The key travels in the link, the server never receives it, and every version carries your signature.

No accounts

Whoever gets the link opens it in a browser, with no sign-up.

You decide how long

Up to 15 days without activity, and you can stop sharing at any time. Your document stays in your Tisty.

There are exceptions: after a serious report or an order, a document stops being served and may be kept up to 180 days or what the order asks, and whoever reports it can get the link to the operator. The details are in the privacy policy.
